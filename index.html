☣️ Spoiled Meat / Fish Detected! H₂S

High Priority

High concentrations of Hydrogen Sulfide (H₂S) and Ammonia (NH₃) produce intense "rotten egg" stenches and toxic bio-aerosols. The strong smell can cause headaches, nausea, and eye irritation if you stay near the bin.

  • 🚨 Action: Empty the bin right away using gloves and sealed bags.
  • 💨 Ventilation: Open windows or doors near the bin area.
  • 🧼 Clean: Wash the inside of the bin with disinfectant after emptying.

Your dashboard is currently running in Full Local Simulation Mode. If you want your physical NodeMCU / ESP8266 to sync with this dashboard in real-time, your Firebase database requires public read/write rules.

1. Go to your Firebase Console:

Navigate to Build > Realtime Database > Rules.

2. Paste these rules and click "Publish":
{
  "rules": {
    ".read": true,
    ".write": true
  }
}

Once published, this dashboard will automatically sync live with your physical dustbin hardware!
